Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 5 August 2026

Laina is operated by Aris Digital Ltd. This policy explains, in plain language, what personal information Laina handles, why it is needed, who receives it, and the choices available to you.

The short version: Laina uses your WhatsApp number and the information needed to keep a shared agreement record. We do not move money, read your other chats, access your contact list, sell your personal information, or use it for targeted advertising.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Aris Digital Ltd decides why and how personal information is processed for Laina and is the organisation responsible for it. Privacy questions and rights requests can be sent to info@aris-digital.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Identity and contact information: your WhatsApp phone number or the account identifier WhatsApp sends to Laina, your WhatsApp display name, and the name and number supplied for the other person.
  • Agreement information: amount, currency, purpose or note, payment dates, instalment plan, status, requested changes, extensions, cancellations, problems, resolutions, and other details you choose to submit.
  • Activity and evidence information: confirmations, declared payments, responses, timestamps, reminders, delivery records, event history, and integrity-check data.
  • Report and support information: an email address when you request a report, the report request and delivery status, and messages you send to support.
  • Technical information: WhatsApp message and delivery identifiers, message-processing status, error and security records, and limited service logs. We may infer a broad country or time zone from a phone identifier so dates and reminders make sense.

3. Where the information comes from

We receive information directly from you, from the other participant in an agreement, from Meta/WhatsApp when messages and Flow responses are delivered, and from actions Laina records automatically.

If another person enters your name or number, Laina may contact you to explain the proposed agreement and ask for your response. You can decline an agreement that is not yours or is not accurate.

4. Information Laina does not need

  • Laina does not ask for your banking password, bank login, payment-card details, contact list, precise location, national identity document, or credit score.
  • Laina cannot read your unrelated WhatsApp conversations.
  • Laina does not hold, collect, or transfer the money covered by an agreement.

Please do not put sensitive information that Laina does not ask for into a purpose, note, support email, or ordinary WhatsApp message.

5. Why we use personal information

We use personal information only where we have a lawful reason. Depending on the situation and the law that applies, this may be to provide the service you requested, carry out our agreement with you, pursue legitimate interests that do not override your rights, comply with law, or act with your consent.

Those purposes include:

  • creating, confirming, updating, and displaying the shared agreement record;
  • sending reminders, status messages, and action requests;
  • recording corrections, reported payments, problems, and resolutions;
  • generating and emailing a report when an authorised participant asks for one;
  • providing support, preventing misuse, securing Laina, and fixing failures;
  • meeting legal, regulatory, accounting, and evidence-preservation duties; and
  • producing aggregated service statistics that are designed not to identify an individual.

We do not use Laina agreement data for targeted advertising and we do not sell personal information.

6. Information shared with the other participant

Laina is a shared record. Agreement details and relevant actions are shown or messaged to the other participant so both people can see what has been proposed, confirmed, paid, changed, questioned, or resolved. Do not submit something you are not authorised to share with that person.

7. Service providers and other recipients

We use carefully selected providers to run Laina. They process information for the stated purpose of providing their part of the service:

  • Meta and WhatsApp — WhatsApp messages, templates, delivery status, and WhatsApp Flows;
  • Supabase — operational database, access controls, and managed recovery;
  • Railway — hosting and running the Laina backend;
  • Resend — delivery of requested evidence-report emails; and
  • website, security, and professional advisers — only where needed to host public information, secure the service, obtain advice, or respond to a lawful matter.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, to protect a person's safety or legal rights, or in connection with a genuine corporate reorganisation. We do not give agreement data to data brokers or advertisers.

8. Processing in other countries

WhatsApp and our service providers may process personal information outside the country where you live, including where their infrastructure or support teams operate. Cross-border processing remains subject to applicable data-protection requirements. Contact us if you want information about the providers or safeguards relevant to your information.

9. Automated reminders and status changes

Laina uses rules to send reminders and to move a record between states when a deadline passes or a user takes an action. These rules help keep the shared record current. They do not assess your creditworthiness, decide whether you legally owe money, blacklist you, or make a court decision. If a status appears wrong, use Laina's problem or support route so it can be reviewed.

10. How we protect information

We use measures such as encrypted connections, restricted service access, database access controls, backups, redacted application logging, and a linked digital fingerprint for each saved event that helps detect later alteration. Only people and providers who need access to operate or protect Laina should receive it.

No system can guarantee perfect security. If a security incident affects your information, we will investigate and notify affected people and regulators where the law requires it.

11. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected and for applicable legal, security, dispute, and evidence-preservation requirements.

  • Agreement and event records are kept while the agreement is active and afterwards for the period reasonably needed to provide the shared history, answer disputes, and meet legal obligations.
  • Operational message records, duplicate-prevention identifiers, logs, and temporary copies held by the email provider are kept for shorter operational periods unless a failure, security incident, legal hold, or lawful request requires longer retention.
  • Aggregated statistics may be kept longer when they no longer identify you.

When retention is no longer justified, information is deleted or de-identified where appropriate. You may ask us how the current retention rule applies to a particular record.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you and request access to it;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to delete, restrict, or stop particular processing;
  • object to processing based on particular grounds;
  • withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing;
  • request a usable copy of information where that right applies; and
  • complain to the relevant data-protection regulator.

Email info@aris-digital.com to make a request. We may need to verify that you control the relevant WhatsApp number before disclosing or changing a record. We will explain if a request cannot be completed in full because the record also belongs to another participant, must be preserved by law, or is needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.

13. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can investigate. You may also complain to the regulator that applies to your situation, including the Data Protection Authority of Zimbabwe through POTRAZ or the Information Regulator of South Africa. Contacting us first does not take away your right to contact a regulator.

14. WhatsApp and copies held by other people

Meta/WhatsApp applies its own privacy policy to your WhatsApp account and messages. Messages already delivered may remain on your device, the other participant's device, or within WhatsApp under its rules. Another participant may also keep a report or screenshot they lawfully received. Aris Digital cannot erase copies it does not control.

15. Public website

The public Laina website does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioural advertising. Website and content-delivery providers may still receive normal technical requests, such as an IP address, browser information, and the page requested, to deliver and protect the site.

16. Children

Laina is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly offer the service to children. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information so we can investigate.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Laina, our providers, or the law changes. We will post the new version here and change the date above. If a change materially affects active users, we will take reasonable steps to notify them.

18. Contact

For privacy questions, complaints, or rights requests, email info@aris-digital.com. Please do not email passwords, bank login details, or full payment-card details.
